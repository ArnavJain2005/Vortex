## What these results support

- Reading a has large, repeatable APE and blind-window deficits on MH_03–05 and V1_01. Reading b is better on most Machine Hall copies, but does not reliably beat the floor on V1_02/03. A single pooled mean conceals both behaviors.
- Blind RMSE is much larger than the measured blind growth on the worst reading-a cases. This is consistent with error already present at sensor failure, but the aggregate metrics cannot isolate the starting pose error.
- Step L1's seeds 7–9 repeat patterns on the same six sequences. They are a robustness check, not an independent validation set.
